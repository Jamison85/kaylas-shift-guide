# Till: seven-second acting test

**Scope:** Separate experimental branch only. Never merge, publish, deploy, or change the real Casey's shift-guide startup.

## Goal
Prove Till can **act continuously** in a believable miniature office, instead of behaving like full-body PNG artwork slid around by CSS. Do not rebuild the existing onboarding. Make one exceptional seven-second performance shot and show it before doing anything else.

**Reference:** Extract `till_first_connection_codex_package.zip` in this branch. Use the APPROVED character, wardrobe, expressions, office identity and details. The old exported Hello-Till HTML is a storytelling/reference artifact, NOT a movement system to reuse.

## Composition
Portrait 9:16, phone-first. Fixed elevated three-quarter overhead camera, roughly 35–45 degrees down into a small office. Consistent human-to-furniture scale; Till's feet planted on the office floor; physically accurate contacts, occlusion, shadows and desk placement. Same tall slender older blond character with blue eyes, black glasses, red Casey's polo and pens in his chest pocket in every frame. No new random characters, no portals, no falling boxes, no UI overlays. Viewer is a giant above the room.

## One unbroken seven-second acting shot
- **0.0–1.6:** Till absorbed in writing on clipboard by desk. His writing wrist and forearm move independently; other hand steadies paper; relaxed breathing and weight.
- **1.6–2.4:** Slight odd tremor in cup and paper. He arrests pen halfway through a word; delayed, subtle body reaction.
- **2.4–4.7:** His eyes travel upward first. Then head, neck, shoulders, and weight adjust with anticipation. Do NOT pop instantly into another pose or slide his feet.
- **4.7–6.3:** He reaches genuine eye contact with giant viewer overhead. His expression changes from curiosity to bafflement. Natural blink and small recoil.
- **6.3–7.0:** Adjusts one side of glasses, wordless little 'hm?' and a slight hold. End here. No script/choice/chaos yet.

Sound only: tiny pen scratches, low room hum, one soft mysterious rumble. No music, narration, lip-sync, subtitles or speech bubbles. Body language MUST carry the scene.

## Production gates
**Acceptable:** Properly articulated 3D character rig and rendered scene, OR one continuous high-quality reference-guided image-to-video character-animation clip. Every part must move naturally and remain physically consistent.

**Hard FAIL:** Replacing/cross-fading whole PNG characters; moving or warping an entire flat sprite; using bouncing or camera shake instead of acting; static slideshow; changing face/costume/proportions; generic replacement character. If an actual articulated model or an image-to-video service/reference is unavailable, STOP and specify the missing asset/service and exact way to get it. Do not make another pseudo-animation.

## Deliverables and acceptance
One **playable seven-second shot**, 9:16, suitable for 390×844 phone. Report technique and source references, plus honest limitations. Test continuous head/eye/arm movement; no pose popping; contact, shadows, and room perspective; viewer eye contact. The test fails if it looks like a cardboard cutout.

**No full intro, no merge, no deployment until this one shot earns approval.**
